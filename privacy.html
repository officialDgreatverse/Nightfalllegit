Privacy Policy

Nightfall by Greatman Nyenwe (dgreatverse) · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Overview

Nightfall is a top-down mobile shooter game owned and developed by Greatman Nyenwe (dgreatverse). We don't require an account and we don't collect your name, email, or contact details.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores, ranks, level progress, your chosen character name, and remaining ad weapon or shield matches are stored locally on your device. We don't receive this data.

Offline multiplayer

Multiplayer uses a local hotspot / Wi-Fi connection between nearby devices. Game data goes directly between those devices and does not pass through our servers.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows optional rewarded ads provided by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). You choose to watch them, for example to unlock an ad weapon or shield for a few matches. Unity may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, device information, and ad interaction data to show, measure, and improve ads. We don't control this data. See Unity's policy: Unity Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

Permissions

The app may request internet, network, and Wi-Fi permissions. These are used for multiplayer, ads, and update checks.

Children

Nightfall is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Updates and downloads

Update checks and APK downloads are served through GitHub. GitHub may log standard request data under its own privacy statement.
