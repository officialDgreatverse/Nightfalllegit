Privacy Policy

Nightfall by Appsite · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Overview

Nightfall is a top-down mobile shooter game. We don't require an account and we don't collect your name, email, or contact details.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores, and ranks are stored locally on your device. We don't receive them.

Offline multiplayer

Multiplayer uses a local hotspot / Wi-Fi connection between nearby devices. Game data goes directly between those devices and does not pass through our servers.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The ad provider may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to show and measure ads. See their policy: Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your Android settings.

Permissions

The app may request network and Wi-Fi permissions for multiplayer and ads.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Updates and downloads

Update checks and APK downloads are served through GitHub. GitHub may log standard request data under its own privacy statement.
